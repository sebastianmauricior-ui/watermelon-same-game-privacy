1. Information We May Collect

Watermelon Same Game may process certain information necessary to provide the game's features, display advertising, process in-app purchases, maintain the operation of the game, and improve the application.

Depending on the features used and the third-party services integrated into the game, information that may be processed includes:

  • Device information.
  • Advertising identifiers.
  • Information related to interaction with advertisements.
  • Technical and diagnostic information.
  • Information related to in-app purchases and transactions.

Watermelon Same Game does not directly request passwords, credit card numbers, banking information, personal contacts, or precise location.

The game does not require users to create an account and does not directly collect or store a player's name for an online profile or leaderboard.

2. Data Stored Locally

Watermelon Same Game may store information locally on the user's device to preserve game progress and settings.

This information may include:

  • Score.
  • In-game coins.
  • Game progress.
  • The state and positions of fruits inside the game container.
  • Game settings and preferences.
  • Other information needed to preserve gameplay, where applicable.

This information is used to provide the game's features and preserve the player's progress.

Locally stored game data may be deleted if the user uninstalls the application, clears the application's data through the device settings, or uses applicable reset functionality within the game.

If locally stored data is deleted, Watermelon Same Game may not be able to restore the previously stored score, coins, progress, fruit positions, or settings.

3. Advertising

Watermelon Same Game uses Google AdMob to display advertisements.

The application may display:

  • Banner advertisements.
  • Interstitial advertisements.
  • Rewarded advertisements.

Rewarded advertisements may provide in-game rewards after the user completes the required advertisement.

Google AdMob and its advertising partners may process information related to the device, advertising identifiers, IP address, interactions with advertisements and the application, diagnostic information, and other information necessary to provide, measure, maintain, and improve advertising services.

The information processed by Google AdMob is subject to Google's applicable privacy policies and terms.

4. In-App Purchases

Watermelon Same Game may allow users to make optional in-app purchases where these features are available.

In-app purchases are processed through Google Play Billing, which is provided by Google Play.

The game may receive information necessary to verify a purchase and provide the corresponding in-game items or benefits, such as information associated with the purchase and its status.

Watermelon Same Game does not directly collect or store users' credit card numbers, bank account information, or other payment credentials used to complete purchases through Google Play.

Payment information and transactions are handled through Google Play according to Google's applicable policies and terms.
